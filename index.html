Verify your download

Optional, but recommended.

  1. Open PowerShell in the folder with the downloaded file.
  2. Run Get-FileHash .\OgorodTalk.exe -Algorithm SHA256
  3. Compare the result with the SHA256 shown above. They must match exactly.
